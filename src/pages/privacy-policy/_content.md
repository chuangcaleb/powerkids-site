## 3. Information We Collect

When you visit our website or interact with our digital advertisements, certain technical data may be collected automatically, including:

Our kindergarten programmes serve young children. We do not collect personal data directly from children. ## 5. Legal Basis for Processing

Under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA) of Malaysia, we process your personal data based on the following grounds:

- **Consent** — where you have voluntarily provided your information through our website forms, social media lead forms, or WhatsApp channels
- **Contractual necessity** — where processing is required to take steps at your request (e.g. processing a child’s enrolment or evaluating a job application)
- **Legitimate interests** — where we have a legitimate reason to process your data and it does not override your rights (e.g. improving our programmes, maintaining website security)
- **Legal obligation** — where we are required to process data to comply with applicable Malaysian laws and educational regulations

## 6. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share your information in the following limited circumstances:

- **Service providers** — with trusted third-party vendors who assist us in operating our website, managing enquiries, running advertising campaigns, or delivering communications (e.g. website hosting, CRM systems, messaging platforms). These parties are bound by confidentiality obligations and may only process your data as instructed by us.
- **Educational and enrichment partners** — with authorised curriculum providers, enrichment partners, or school photographers/vendors strictly in the context of programme delivery and school activities, with appropriate safeguards and parental consent in place.
- **Legal requirements** — where we are required to disclose your information to comply with a legal obligation, court order, or government/ministry request.
- **Business transfer** — in the event of a merger, acquisition, or transfer of centre ownership, your information may be transferred to the relevant successor organisation.

## 7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as is necessary for the purposes stated in this Privacy Policy, or as required by law. Specifically:

- **Enrolment & Open Day enquiries:** retained for the duration of the relevant intake or academic cycle, unless your child enrols with us, in which case records are retained for as long as necessary to fulfill educational, administrative, and legal obligations.

## 8. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies and tracking pixels — small data files stored on your browser — to improve your experience, measure ad performance, and help us understand how visitors use our site.

We use the following types of cookies:

- **Essential cookies** — necessary for the website to function correctly (e.g. form submission)
- **Analytics cookies** — to understand visitor behaviour and improve our website (e.g. Google Analytics)
- **Marketing and advertising pixels** — to measure the effectiveness of our social media and search campaigns (e.g. Meta Pixel, TikTok Pixel, Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics, etc) and deliver relevant information to prospective parents
- **Preference cookies** — to remember your settings and choices

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of our website. For more information on managing cookies, visit [www.allaboutcookies.org](https://www.allaboutcookies.org).

## 9. Your Rights Under the PDPA

As a data subject under the Malaysian Personal Data Protection Act 2010, you have the following rights:

- **Right to access** — you may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
- **Right to correction** — you may request that we correct any inaccurate or incomplete data
- **Right to withdraw consent** — you may withdraw your consent to our use of your data (including marketing or WhatsApp follow-ups) at any time, though this will not affect the lawfulness of processing prior to withdrawal
- **Right to limit processing** — in certain circumstances, you may request that we restrict the processing of your data
- **Right to data portability** — you have the right to request that your personal data be transferred in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format to another service provider, where technically feasible
- **Right to be informed** — you have the right to be informed about how your data is being used, as set out in this Privacy Policy

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us in writing at [powerkidschool@gmail.com](mailto:powerkidschool@gmail.com). We will respond to your request within 21 working days.
